Fig. 4About this design · Cartographer’s note

The name came first. This site has always been called Doops in the Wind, and every design before it treated the wind as scenery. I took the name literally instead and charted it: the plate at the top is a real streamline field, 144 lines traced through a modelled wind over the open steppe, colored by speed, with Astana marked at its true coordinates, because that is where the work is done.

Two things steered the hand. Ted Chiang’s Exhalation, which Dina calls the best expression of being alive or existing, imagines thought as a pattern of moving air; a page about thinking in a hard climate ought to look like weather. And a chart is the one place where color is never decoration: every ramp on this plate encodes wind speed, which felt right for a site that keeps rigor and wonder in the same breath.
