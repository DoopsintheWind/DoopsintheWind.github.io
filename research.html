The Research

Contested things

Fig. 1. Chart of the research holdings. Sheet 2 of 6 · MMXXVI

I study how people interpret contested things: what they take to be true, who they trust and why, and how they make sense of the choices they're given. The work runs across meaning-making in authoritarian politics, the management of visible and invisible lives, and what actually moves attitudes when institutions try to persuade. A new strand is just beginning: AI in higher education, starting with fieldwork done with AI as a working partner.
