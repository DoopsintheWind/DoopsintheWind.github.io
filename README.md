# Dinara (Dina) Pisareva

Personal site of Dinara (Dina) Pisareva, Assistant Professor of Political Science at Nazarbayev University, Astana. Live at https://doopsinthewind.github.io/

## Living a Good Woman's Life: a Study in Qazaqstan (Kazakhstan)

A life-course study of women in Kazakhstan: a hundred and twenty life-history interviews in Qazaq and Russian across five regions. Gender studies, qualitative research, Central Asia.

https://doopsinthewind.github.io/good-womans-life/

Research team: Dinara Pisareva, Aruzhan Seidaliyeva, Dilnaz Mukhtarkhanova, Akerke Mukhtarkyzy (Nazarbayev University).
